# Modern English Pokémon binder-source search

Checked 2026-10-15. Four distinct English-language YouTube sources positively listed 3840×2160 formats in the public metadata probe. Two short excerpts were acquired, ffprobe-checked at 3840×2160, and inspected at full frame size. Wv8WJmU3xQc is the strongest card-crop source; xFEyeGtsIS0 adds a crowded physical-page scene. Neither source provides verified card identities or independent camera-evaluation data.

## Best inspected sources

1. [Evan's Collection — “Reacting To My Viewers' Pokémon Binders (Binder Ideas)”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wv8WJmU3xQc), uploaded 2026-08-07. The 35–50 second excerpt shows two distinct binder-page views in retained frames at approximately clip-relative 5 and 11 seconds. One view contains about 18 cards across two facing pages; the other has around 10 visible card faces. Those are visual counts only. Many faces are large (roughly 450–650×600–850 source pixels by inspection), and page boundaries remain visible. The pages are close to top-down and stable, with sleeve glare on some cards; one is tilted. English and Japanese text both appear, so do not treat this source as English-only card imagery. The video edits between the host and viewer-submitted page footage. Keep the two page frames together in one source group.

2. [HLD Cards & Collectibles — “Pokemon Binders but they get increasingly more ridiculous”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xFEyeGtsIS0), uploaded 2026-06-25. A 20-second excerpt at 155–175 seconds shows one physical binder page on a trade-show table. The page has about 30 visible pockets in a 6×5 layout. It is useful for dense-page localization, but its individual cards are smaller (roughly 250–320 pixels wide), softer, and affected by sleeve reflections. A hand, display cases, channel watermark, and captions also appear. The sequence mostly repeats the same page, so count one useful unique page view rather than the repeated frames.

The clips and selected full-resolution frames are in `datasets/sources/videos/binder-search-pokemon-modern/`. Frame details, source-group identifiers, SHA-256 hashes, dimensions, and clip-relative times are in the two `*-mined/manifest.json` files. The clip-relative frame times do not assert exact source-time alignment: YouTube section cuts can shift to nearby keyframes. The requested source windows are known; exact frame offsets within them are approximate. Three 3840×2160 JPEG frames are retained in total. No cards were labeled, and exact catalog or printing identities remain unresolved.

## Additional verified leads

A 30–50 second test window showed unrelated fishing footage, so it is a metadata-qualified lead only; no binder-frame quality claim is made. [Nidovin Collects — “Pokemon 30th Anniversary Binder Collection - EARLY OPENING (4K)”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bWfct0Vx0YM), uploaded 2026-09-15, positively lists 3840×2160 at 25 fps. Its inspected 70–85 second “binder overview” window showed the binder cover and empty pockets, followed by booster packs, without card fronts. It is not recommended for card-crop acquisition based on that window.

The machine-readable candidate list and format records are in [`resources/benchmarks/binder_search_pokemon_modern.json`](../../resources/benchmarks/binder_search_pokemon_modern.json). Positive listed formats were verified on 2026-10-15 with yt-dlp; its warning says missing formats may be omitted without a JavaScript runtime, so only positive listings support qualification. Excerpt output was ffprobe-checked. Public viewing access does not establish reuse permission; reuse basis is unknown for every source. No accounts, cookies, proxies, or full-video downloads were used.
